AI Pet - Controls
=================

Mouse move        Pet follows your cursor whenever it doesn't need
                   anything more urgent (food/water/toy/rest).
Click the pet      Pet it! Boosts happiness (small cooldown between
                   clicks so spam-clicking doesn't max it out instantly).
Click "Toy"        Drop (or re-throw) the toy at a random spot on the
button (top-right)  ground. Once the pet gets bored enough, it'll go
                   play with it - playing also raises happiness.
Close window (X)   Quit. Progress (the pet's trained brain + personality)
                   auto-saves every 30 seconds while playing, and saves
                   again on close.
Esc                Also quits, in case the window's close button doesn't
                   register on your window manager.

Needs (bars, top-left): hunger, thirst, boredom, tiredness. Each rises over
time; the pet will automatically go handle whichever is most urgent once it
crosses its threshold (or, for tiredness, just stop and rest in place).
Happiness is different - it's not something the pet seeks out on its own,
it just drifts down when ignored and goes up from play/petting.

Not implemented yet (ideas for later):
- Feed or water the pet directly by clicking the food/water items
- Keyboard movement or jumping
