AI Pet - Controls
=================

Main menu
---------
Type a name        Enter your pet's name (letters/numbers/space/-/_ only).
                    If a save already exists under that exact name, it's
                    loaded instead of starting fresh.
Start / Enter       Begin playing.
Quit                Close the app.

Playing
-------
Right-click          Opens a context menu at your cursor with "Call" (send
                     the pet here), "Pet" (pet it, from wherever you
                     right-clicked - see below for why petting moved off
                     left-click), and "Fetch" (command a fetch round right
                     now - see Fetch mini-game below; needs still come
                     first if any are urgent). Click elsewhere or press Esc
                     to dismiss without acting. The pet does NOT follow
                     your mouse on its own - use Call to bring it somewhere.
Click the food       Feed the pet directly - instantly satisfies hunger,
                     wherever the pet currently is.
Click the water      Water the pet directly - instantly satisfies thirst,
                     wherever the pet currently is.
Click the toybox     Opens a toy-picker menu ("Ball" and "Bone" so far, more
                     may come later). Picking a toy that's not out brings it
                     out, at rest, near the box; picking a different one
                     while one's already out swaps it. Picking the SAME toy
                     again removes it from the field. Ball and Bone play
                     identically - only the look's different for now.
Click the toy        Once a toy is out and sitting still, click it to throw
                     it - which half of the toy you click sets the
                     direction (click the right half to throw left, and
                     vice versa), and how close to the edge you click sets
                     the power (center = weak, edge = strong). Click it
                     again once it settles to re-throw. Once the pet gets
                     bored enough it'll go play with it too - playing also
                     raises happiness.
Esc                  Opens the pause menu.

Why petting moved to the right-click menu: the pet is drawn on top of
everything, so a left-click on it would always win over whatever's
underneath - like a toy it just fetched back to you. Right-click "Pet"
works from anywhere, so it's never blocked by what's on the ground.

Pause menu (Esc while playing)
-------------------------------
Resume (or Esc again)  Back to the game.
Save                    Save right now, instead of waiting for autosave.
Load                    Pick a different saved pet by name to switch to
                        (your current pet is saved first).
Back to Main Menu       Save and return to the main menu.

Progress (the pet's trained brain + personality) auto-saves every 30
seconds while playing, and also saves on Save/Load/Back to Main
Menu/closing the window.

Needs (bars, top-left): hunger, thirst, boredom, tiredness. Each rises over
time; the pet will automatically go handle whichever is most urgent once it
crosses its threshold - food/water/the toy for the first three, and its bed
for tiredness: it walks over and actually sleeps there (a "ZzZzZz" bubble
while it does) rather than just stopping wherever it happens to be.
Happiness is different - it's not something the pet seeks out on its own,
it just drifts down when ignored and goes up from play/petting.

Idle behavior: whenever nothing is urgent and you haven't called it, the pet
doesn't just freeze - it picks something to do on its own: stand around,
wander to a random spot, go nap on the bed (same "ZzZzZz" bubble as urgent
tiredness sleep - it's resting either way), throw a toy for itself and
play, or bring a toy over to your cursor for a game of fetch. If no toy is
out but the toybox is there, it may go grab one out first. Which it tends
to pick is shaped by its personality (randomized per pet, currently not
shown in the UI) - and fetch specifically can be TAUGHT: pet it (via the
right-click menu) shortly after it brings a toy back and that reinforces
fetch as a favorite, a little more each time; ignore it and that preference
fades again over a few minutes, so it settles back to playing alone. An
urgent need always interrupts whatever it's doing; a Call does too, but a
pending Fetch command just waits for a Call or urgent need to finish first.

Fetch mini-game: every successful delivery earns coins (top-right counter -
see Currency below), whether the pet decided to fetch on its own or you
commanded it with the right-click "Fetch" option (which chains into
grabbing a toy from the toybox first if none is out). Consecutive
deliveries build a streak - flat for the first couple, then it ramps up
fast, so a short deliberate streak is worth a lot more than one-off
fetches. Anything that breaks the chain (the pet getting distracted by
something else, or an interrupted delivery) resets the streak to zero.
Commanding a round pays more than letting the pet decide on its own.

Currency: shown as a coin counter, top-right. It belongs to you as the
player, not to any one pet - switching pets (Load) doesn't reset it.
Nothing to spend it on yet; that's planned once cosmetics exist.

Vocalizations: every so often the pet pops up a speech bubble - an
onomatopoeia plus an emoji for the emotion behind it. A happy bark when
petted, called over, or starting to play; a whine if it wants something and
you've been slow to notice; a growl instead, not a whine, if it's been
neglected badly enough to be genuinely upset; a lonely howl if it's just
been alone a while with nothing it specifically needs. Only one shows at a
time, on a cooldown, so it won't spam - and none of them show while it's
asleep (the ZzZzZz bubble takes over instead).

Not implemented yet (ideas for later):
- More toy types from the toybox - a tug rope is planned (hold one end and
  the pet grabs the other for tug-of-war), plus a cat-chasing toy once a
  cat companion exists
- Keyboard movement or jumping
- A "Load" browser directly from the main menu (right now you have to
  retype a saved pet's exact name there to resume it)
